# SOP-PZ-001: Personnel Exclusion Zones — Furnace Operations

## Document ID
**SOP-Personnel-Zones** | Revision 3.0 | Metal Foundry Safety

## Description
During furnace charging, tapping, and active melting phases, strict personnel exclusion zones must be enforced around the electric arc furnace (EAF), induction furnace, and associated equipment. Personnel detected within exclusion zones during active operations represent a **critical safety violation** requiring immediate intervention. Visual inspection systems must flag any human presence within the defined perimeter.

## Visual Identification Criteria
- Human figure or silhouette visible within the furnace bay during charging or tapping
- Personnel not wearing full foundry PPE (aluminized suit, face shield, heat-resistant gloves)
- Workers positioned between the furnace and the charging crane path
- Personnel on the furnace platform during active melt or charging phase
- Any person within the spatter/splash zone without overhead protection

## Exclusion Zone Definitions

### Zone A — Critical Exclusion (0–10 meters from furnace shell)
- **NO PERSONNEL** permitted during charging, melting, or tapping phases
- Exception: Furnace operator in shielded control pulpit only
- Violations are **CRITICAL** severity — immediate work stoppage required
- Risk: Direct exposure to molten splash, extreme radiant heat (>500 W/m²), fume inhalation, arc flash

### Zone B — Restricted Access (10–20 meters from furnace shell)
- Personnel permitted only with full foundry PPE and specific task authorization
- Maximum exposure time: 15 minutes continuous
- Must maintain visual contact with furnace status indicators
- Risk: Radiant heat exposure, fume accumulation in low-ventilation areas, falling debris

### Zone C — Controlled Area (20–50 meters from furnace shell)
- Standard foundry PPE required (hard hat, safety glasses, steel-toe boots, hearing protection)
- Normal work activities permitted with awareness of furnace status
- Evacuation route knowledge mandatory
- Risk: Noise exposure, residual fume drift, emergency evacuation path

## Risk Level
**CRITICAL** — Personnel proximity violations during active furnace operations are the leading cause of severe burn injuries and fatalities in metal foundries. Every detection must trigger an immediate response.

## Consequences of Violation
- **Thermal burns**: Radiant heat and molten metal splash cause severe to fatal burns
- **Fume inhalation**: Acute metal fume fever, respiratory distress, long-term lung damage
- **Arc flash injury**: UV and IR radiation causing eye damage and skin burns
- **Crush hazard**: Overhead crane and charge bucket movement in the furnace bay
- **Regulatory action**: OSHA citations, facility shutdown orders, criminal negligence charges in case of injury

## Immediate Actions — Personnel Detected in Zone A
1. **ACTIVATE AUDIBLE ALARM** — Sound the furnace bay evacuation horn immediately
2. **HALT ALL CHARGING AND CRANE OPERATIONS** — No material movement until zone is clear
3. **IDENTIFY the individual** — Determine if they are authorized furnace crew or unauthorized entry
4. **DIRECT PERSONNEL TO EXIT** via the nearest designated safe route (away from furnace, crane path, and tapping side)
5. **VERIFY ZONE IS CLEAR** — Visual and camera confirmation before resuming operations
6. **LOG THE INCIDENT** — Record timestamp, personnel identity, duration of exposure, and corrective action

## Immediate Actions — Personnel Detected in Zone B Without PPE
1. Stop work in the affected area
2. Direct personnel to PPE station before re-entry
3. Log as a safety observation and notify shift supervisor
4. Review access authorization for the individual

## Recommended Response
- **Any person in Zone A during active operations** → CRITICAL. Immediate halt. No exceptions.
- **Unauthorized person in Zone B** → WARNING. Halt nearby operations. Escort out.
- **Missing PPE in Zone C** → WARNING. Correct immediately. Safety coaching required.
- **Repeated violations by same individual** → Suspension from furnace bay access. Management review.

## Escalation Conditions
- Person detected in Zone A during charging or tapping → **EMERGENCY STOP ALL OPERATIONS**
- Person on furnace platform during melt → **EMERGENCY STOP, initiate rescue standby**
- Unauthorized personnel in furnace bay (no badge/PPE) → **SECURITY RESPONSE, halt operations**
- Zone barriers displaced or missing → **HALT OPERATIONS until barriers are restored**
- Camera/detection system offline for Zone A → **Manual spotter required, reduce operations to minimum**

## Zone Enforcement Equipment
- [ ] Physical barriers and chain gates at Zone A boundary: Intact and closed
- [ ] Warning signage (multilingual): Visible at all entry points
- [ ] Audible alarm system: Tested and functional
- [ ] Flashing light beacons at Zone A boundary: Operational
- [ ] Camera coverage: All Zone A entry points monitored
- [ ] Emergency escape routes: Clear and illuminated
