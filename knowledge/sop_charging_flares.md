# SOP-CF-001: Charging Flare and Thermal Reaction Response

## Document ID
**SOP-Charging-Flares** | Revision 2.1 | Metal Foundry Operations

## Description
During scrap metal charging into electric arc furnaces (EAF) or induction furnaces, intense white/orange light bursts ("charging flares") occur as a thermal reaction between moisture-laden scrap and molten metal. These flares produce extreme radiant heat (>1500°C at source), UV radiation, dense metallic fumes, and airborne particulate matter. Charging flares are visually identifiable as sudden, intense brightness filling the furnace bay area, often accompanied by visible smoke plumes and sparking.

## Visual Identification Criteria
- Extreme white or orange light saturating camera field of view (overexposure)
- Visible spark showers radiating outward from furnace opening
- Dense smoke or fume plumes rising above the furnace
- Glow reflected on surrounding structures, walls, and overhead crane
- Molten splash or spatter visible around the charging zone

## Risk Level
**CRITICAL** — Thermal reactions during charging are the highest-risk phase of furnace operation. Uncontrolled flares indicate potential issues with scrap moisture content, fume extraction system capacity, or charging procedure deviation.

## Consequences
- **Fume exposure**: Metallic fumes (zinc, manganese, iron oxide) pose acute respiratory hazard to personnel in the furnace bay
- **Thermal radiation burns**: Personnel within 15 meters may suffer radiant heat injuries
- **Fire hazard**: Sparks and molten spatter can ignite nearby combustible materials, hydraulic hoses, or cable insulation
- **Equipment damage**: Overhead crane, electrode arms, and roof structure may sustain thermal damage from uncontrolled flares
- **Explosion risk**: Sealed containers, wet scrap, or trapped moisture in scrap can cause steam explosions during charging

## Immediate Actions
1. **Verify fume extraction system is at FULL CAPACITY** — secondary ventilation must be active during all charging operations
2. **Confirm charging crane position** — crane must be at safe retract distance before next charge bucket is released
3. **Check personnel clearance** — no personnel within the designated exclusion zone (minimum 10 meters from furnace)
4. **Monitor roof extraction hood** — ensure hood is in lowered (capture) position with draft verified
5. **Inspect scrap moisture level** — if flare intensity exceeds normal baseline, halt charging and inspect remaining scrap for moisture or sealed containers

## Recommended Response by Severity

### Normal Charging Flare (Expected)
- Bright light visible but contained within furnace bay envelope
- Fume extraction capturing >90% of visible emissions
- **Action**: Continue operations. Log observation. No intervention needed.

### Abnormal Charging Flare (Elevated Intensity)
- Light intensity causes camera overexposure or sensor saturation
- Visible fume escape beyond extraction hood capture zone
- Sparks reaching beyond the normal spatter zone
- **Action**: Reduce charge bucket size for next charge. Increase extraction fan speed to maximum. Notify furnace operator. Verify scrap quality for next bucket.

### Extreme Thermal Reaction (Emergency)
- Sustained intense flare lasting >15 seconds
- Molten material ejection outside containment area
- Fume extraction system visibly overwhelmed
- **Action**: HALT CHARGING IMMEDIATELY. Activate emergency ventilation. Clear all personnel from furnace bay. Notify shift supervisor and safety officer. Do not resume until root cause is identified.

## Escalation Conditions
- Flare duration exceeds 15 seconds → **HALT CHARGING, notify supervisor**
- Fume extraction differential pressure drops below threshold → **EMERGENCY VENTILATION PROTOCOL**
- Any molten splash beyond containment barriers → **EMERGENCY STOP, area evacuation**
- Repeated abnormal flares (>2 per heat) → **Suspend operations for scrap yard review**
- Camera/sensor damage from thermal load → **Replace sensor, review placement standard**

## Equipment Verification Checklist
- [ ] Primary fume extraction fan: Running at rated capacity
- [ ] Secondary ventilation: Active and verified
- [ ] Baghouse differential pressure: Within operating range
- [ ] Roof hood position: Lowered to capture position
- [ ] Charging crane: Position verified at safe retract distance
- [ ] Exclusion zone barriers: In place and signage visible
- [ ] Emergency shower/eyewash: Accessible within 30 seconds of furnace bay
