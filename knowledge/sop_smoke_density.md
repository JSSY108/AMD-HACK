# SOP-SD-001: Smoke and Fume Density Monitoring — Furnace Operations

## Document ID
**SOP-Smoke-Density** | Revision 1.5 | Metal Foundry Environmental Safety

## Description
Dense smoke and metallic fume emissions during furnace operations indicate combustion of contaminants in scrap (oil, paint, plastics, rubber), incomplete fume capture by extraction systems, or abnormal melt chemistry. Visible smoke density beyond normal operating baseline is a leading indicator of environmental exceedance and worker exposure risk. Visual inspection systems must assess smoke opacity and plume behavior.

## Visual Identification Criteria
- Dense gray, white, or dark smoke visible above furnace or escaping from bay openings
- Smoke obscuring visibility within the furnace bay (reduced camera clarity)
- Plume drift toward occupied work areas
- Smoke escaping from roof gaps, doors, or wall openings instead of through extraction ductwork
- Visible condensation or particulate fallout on nearby surfaces

## Risk Level
**WARNING to CRITICAL** — Depending on density and composition. Light haze is normal during charging. Dense, dark smoke indicates contaminant combustion or extraction system failure.

## Consequences
- **Worker health**: Metal fume fever, respiratory irritation, chronic lung disease from repeated exposure
- **Environmental violation**: Opacity exceedance triggers regulatory fines and potential shutdown orders
- **Visibility hazard**: Dense smoke impairs crane operator visibility and general situational awareness
- **Fire risk**: Unburned hydrocarbons in heavy smoke can ignite upon contact with hot surfaces

## Immediate Actions
1. **Assess extraction system status** — verify all fans running at rated RPM and dampers fully open
2. **Check baghouse differential pressure** — high ΔP indicates plugged filters requiring maintenance
3. **Evaluate scrap quality** — contaminated scrap (oil, paint, plastics) produces excess smoke
4. **If smoke is escaping the building** — activate supplemental ventilation and notify environmental compliance
5. **If smoke reduces bay visibility below safe limits** — halt crane operations until visibility recovers

## Recommended Response
- **Light haze during charging (normal)** → INFO. Log observation. Continue operations.
- **Moderate smoke with visible plume escape** → WARNING. Increase extraction. Review scrap quality.
- **Dense smoke obscuring visibility** → CRITICAL. Halt charging. Activate emergency ventilation. Clear bay.

## Escalation Conditions
- Opacity reading exceeds permitted limit → **Notify environmental compliance immediately**
- Smoke visible from outside the building → **Regulatory reporting required within 1 hour**
- Extraction system failure during melt → **EMERGENCY VENTILATION, reduce furnace power**
- Personnel reporting respiratory irritation → **Evacuate affected area, provide medical evaluation**
